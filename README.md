# SelfFlex

### Personal finance, simplified.

SelfFlex is a personal finance app by Dana Nahmani, designed to make everyday financial tracking feel clear, calm and manageable.

Built for iPhone and iPad, it focuses on helping users understand their spending, upcoming charges and monthly financial picture without unnecessary complexity.

## What SelfFlex focuses on

- everyday expense tracking
- monthly financial overview
- upcoming charges
- credit card billing cycles
- installment tracking
- spending categories
- financial goals
- clear and focused interaction
- Hebrew and English interfaces
- RTL and LTR support

## Product approach

SelfFlex began as a personal solution and evolved through continuous testing, iteration and user feedback.

The product is designed around clarity rather than complexity, presenting useful financial information without turning everyday money management into a complicated system.

## Development

SelfFlex is developed for Apple platforms using Xcode, Swift and SwiftUI.

Development includes product design, implementation, testing, localization, TestFlight beta testing and continued refinement after release.
