# SelfFlex

### Personal finance, simplified.

SelfFlex is a personal finance app designed to make everyday financial tracking feel clear, calm and manageable.

Built for Apple platforms, it focuses on helping users understand their spending, upcoming charges and monthly financial picture without unnecessary complexity.

## What SelfFlex focuses on

- everyday expense tracking
- monthly financial overview
- upcoming charges
- credit card billing cycles
- installment tracking
- spending categories
- financial goals
- clear and focused interaction
- Hebrew and English interfaces
- RTL and LTR support

## Product approach

SelfFlex began as a personal solution and evolved through continuous testing, iteration and user feedback.

The product is designed around clarity rather than complexity — presenting useful financial information without trying to turn everyday money management into a complicated system.

## Platforms

SelfFlex is designed for iPhone and iPad.

## Development

The app is developed using Xcode, Swift and SwiftUI.

Development includes iterative product design, implementation, testing, localization, beta testing through TestFlight and continued refinement after release.

## Source code

The source code for SelfFlex is proprietary and is not publicly distributed.
